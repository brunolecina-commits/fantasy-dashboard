Power rankings & odds

Ranked by rating: half each team's weekly scores so far, half its roster strength. Roster strength is the best legal lineup per game, with today's injuries and with everyone healthy. Odds come from 10,000 simulated seasons; the top 4 make the playoffs.

Standings & season leaderboard

Ranked by record, then total points. One column per week; this week's column is live. All-play is the record a team would have playing everyone every week; schedule luck is real wins minus that.

Luck: +/− vs a full-strength roster

Each finished day, every team's actual points minus what its full roster should have scored if everyone were healthy: the best legal lineup from all players on the roster that day (IR and injured included) whose NBA team played, at ESPN's projected points per game. Injuries, rest days, cold nights and benching mistakes count against you; hot nights count for you. Projections run high for everyone, so "+/− vs league avg" is the column that shows real luck.
